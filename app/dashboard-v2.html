Build capabilities
This build integrates DMR through MMDVM-Host and DMRGateway, custom XLX hosts/ports, saved radio settings, diagnostics, service control, and backup/restore. Light and dark themes are available throughout.
D-Star, other mode gateways, cross-mode audio, physical Nextion firmware, automatic Wi-Fi setup, and independent HDMI kiosks are not implemented in this build. They are not presented as working switches.
First-boot radio and network transmission are disabled. Hardware RX, TX and network operation must be verified on the target Pi.
